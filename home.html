About Budo

A Legacy of Excellence

Founded in 1906, King's College, Budo stands as one of Uganda's oldest and most respected schools, known for academic excellence and strong character formation.

From our historic beginnings to our modern learning environment, we continue to nurture disciplined, future-ready learners guided by the spirit of "Gakyali Mabaga."
